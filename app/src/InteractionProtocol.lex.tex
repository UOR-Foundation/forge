  parameters: [],
  result: String,
  body: "Interaction wire v1 begins with interact and one ASCII space followed by lowercase hex-encoded little-endian uint32 words. The words are profile version, initial state, declared screen count, frame count, four words per frame (screen palette tone motion), edge count, three words per edge (origin event destination), current state, event, canonical audio-consent flag and reduced-motion flag. At most 32 frames and 128 edges are parsed within 8192 ASCII payload bytes and 1024 words. Truncated, noncanonical, oversized or trailing input refuses. The generated model determines admission and coordinated state/output. Success returns ok-space and five uint32 hex words (state screen palette tone motion); exact typed refusals retain their names. The codec supplies no semantic fallback. Caller state persistence, audio consent UI, rendering and production qualification remain separately required."
