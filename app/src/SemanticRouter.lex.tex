definition{
  name: boundary,
  parameters: [],
  result: String,
  body: "This finite R4-compatible proposal interface uses explicit directed semantic relations and integer activation, not content digests or imported relevance claims. A concept matches a feature vocabulary exactly at weight 1000 or through one declared relation of weight 1 through 1000. Direct intent score is weighted by 32001; context provides a smaller ranking contribution only for candidates with positive direct-intent support. This weighting is explicit, not probabilistic confidence; proposal confidence remains zero. The catalog has at most 32 unique feature keys in dependency-first order, with bounded fields; relations have unique directed pairs and at most 128 rows. Intent and context contain at most 32 unique positive concept IDs. Direct-intent concepts unsupported by the selected composition are unresolved; context never creates a candidate. Positive candidates rank by descending integer score and ascending key. Equal top scores expose every tied key as ambiguity. The highest candidate closes all transitive needs and input producers through at most 32 bounded passes and preserves catalog dependency order. Empty rankings abstain. ProposalAdmission independently rejects stale intent or catalog, ambiguity, unresolved concepts, invalid dependencies, permissions, evidence, target, conflicts or budgets. Routing never executes effects, changes feature meanings or grants authority. An alternative router must satisfy the same typed proposal/admission boundary. This model is a bounded explicit-relation adaptation informed by R4 mechanisms, not an imported learned R4 implementation or evidence of general natural-language relevance. Independent held-out judgments, dependency completeness, abstention and latency qualify only the declared catalog and interpretation profile."
}
