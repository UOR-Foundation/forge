definition{
  name: boundary,
  parameters: [],
  result: String,
  body: "A finite queue contains at most 32 unique positive turn identities, including any active head. Every successful mutation increments its unsigned 32-bit revision; stale revisions and exhausted counters refuse. Enqueue, remove, reorder, edit, clear, pause, resume, stop, claim, complete, recovery and failure are explicit operations. Active work stays at the head; edit/remove/reorder cannot change it, and clear removes only pending work. Claim requires an unpaused queue, no active owner and the current stop policy. Completion requires the exact active item and owner. Stop increments its policy and pauses pending dispatch without discarding active work. Resume observes that policy. Failure retains the item and pauses. Explicit recovery by a different owner also retains the item and pauses, and the host must establish abandonment with an exclusive origin lock before invoking it. This pure model validates state transitions, not identities, prompt payloads, durable storage, remote authentication, inference permission, exactly-once external effects or physical work accounting. The adapter must bind payloads to turn identities, commit state and payload changes atomically, and never report a command applied without that commit."
}
